PRD Reviewer

Paste a draft PRD or feature spec. Get the critique engineering would give you, before you send it.

Drop your PRD here or browse files
.txt, .md or .docx · up to 5 MB

What you’ll get

  • Readiness scoreA 0–100 rating of how ready your PRD is to hand to engineering.
  • 11-point scorecardPass, Partial or Fail on problem, metrics, scope, edge cases, risks and more.
  • Vague languageWords like “fast” or “seamless”, rewritten with measurable targets.
  • Missing piecesGaps such as error states, dependencies and out-of-scope items.
  • Testable requirementsWeak requirements, with acceptance criteria you can reuse.
  • Engineering questionsWhat your developers will likely ask, before they ask it.
